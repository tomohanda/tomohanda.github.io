Privacy Policy

This is a personal OAuth application used to synchronize Obsidian notes with my own Google Drive account.

Google Drive Access

The application requests permission to view, create, edit, and delete files in Google Drive for synchronization purposes.

The requested permission may allow access to files outside the Obsidian vault.

Data Handling

Data is processed for the purpose of synchronizing personal Obsidian notes. This page does not represent an official Obsidian or GSync service.

Revoking Access

Google account access can be revoked at any time through Google Account settings.
